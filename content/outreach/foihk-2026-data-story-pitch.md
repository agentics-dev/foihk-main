## Proposed Story

**Hong Kong's family-office evidence gap: seven official figures, two market scopes that should not be confused**

The story uses a new FOIHK-compiled dataset of official public figures to explain the difference between estimated single-family-office activity and Hong Kong's wider asset and wealth management market. FOIHK did not conduct the underlying surveys and must not describe the source figures as proprietary research.

## Suggested Subject Lines

- Data brief: what Hong Kong's 3,380 family-office estimate does and does not measure
- Media resource: seven sourced Hong Kong family-office and wealth-management figures for 2026
- Story idea: why family-office counts and wealth-management AUM should not be combined

## Pitch Draft

Hello [editor name],

Family Office Institute Hong Kong has compiled a reviewable 2026 dataset that puts Hong Kong's most-cited family-office figures beside their original scope and limitations.

InvestHK reported more than 3,380 single-family offices operating in Hong Kong at the end of 2025, approximately HK$12.6 billion in annual operating expenditure, and more than 10,000 direct full-time roles. Separately, the SFC's 2025 survey of 1,316 firms reported HK$42.2 trillion in asset and wealth management AUM and HK$12.9 trillion in private banking and private wealth management assets.

The distinction matters: the InvestHK figures are market estimates for single-family offices, while the SFC figures describe a wider regulated and surveyed market that excludes self-managed entities such as some single-family offices. Treating the figures as one population produces misleading comparisons.

The downloadable dataset gives every figure's period, population or sample, publisher, direct source URL, and limitation. FOIHK can provide a source-led briefing on how these scopes affect reporting about Hong Kong's family-office ecosystem.

Regards,
